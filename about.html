Anna Kin (she/her) is a research-driven artist working with socially engaged art, performance and new media. Her practice explores postcolonial shifts in Central Asia and the Balkans, Baltic, and Caucasus regions, proposing alternative tools for collective healing. Recent presentations include at BLUE OF THE HIGH ZENITS, performance festival curated by Sabrina Bellenzier and Untitled. Tbilisi (Barın Han, Istanbul, 2026), Hotel Continental—Art Space in Exile (Berlin, 2025), LCCA Summer School, Language and its Context (Valmiera, 2024), ARTBAT FEST 11 (Almaty, 2023), group show Displaced Identity in Zwetajewa Center (Freiburg, 2023).
